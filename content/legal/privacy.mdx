This Privacy Policy explains how ScripticX handles personal information when
you use our website and learning platform.

## Information we collect

We may collect:

- account details such as name, username, email address, and authentication
  information;
- learning activity such as exercises, code submissions, progress, class
  participation, and community posts;
- technical information such as IP address, browser type, device information,
  logs, and security events;
- communications sent to support, privacy, or security channels.

The exact information collected depends on the features you use.

## How we use information

We use information to provide and maintain ScripticX, save learning progress,
operate classes and community features, secure accounts, prevent abuse, respond
to requests, and improve the service.

Where required by law, processing is based on contract, legitimate interests,
consent, or compliance with legal obligations.

## Sharing and processors

We may use service providers for hosting, authentication, communications,
analytics, monitoring, or support. They may process information only according
to their agreement with ScripticX.

We may also disclose information when required by law, to protect users and the
service, or as part of a corporate transaction. ScripticX does not sell personal
information.

## Your rights

Depending on applicable law, you may request access, correction, deletion,
restriction, objection, or portability of personal information. You may also
withdraw consent where processing relies on consent.

Some information may need to be retained for security, legal, or operational
reasons. We may verify your identity before completing a request.

## Contact

Privacy requests and questions can be sent to
[our contact form](https://platform.scripticx.org/contact).

Before launch, this section should also identify the legal entity operating
ScripticX, its registered address, applicable representative details, and the
relevant supervisory authority where required.
