## What are cookies?

Cookies are small pieces of data stored by a browser. Similar technologies,
such as local storage, may also remember settings or support application
functionality.

## Cookies we use

ScripticX may use strictly necessary technologies for:

| Purpose | Example |
| --- | --- |
| Authentication | Keeping a signed-in session active |
| Security | Detecting abuse and protecting forms |
| Preferences | Remembering language or interface choices |

Optional analytics or marketing cookies should not be enabled where consent is
required until the user has made a choice.

## Managing cookies

You can remove or block cookies through browser settings. Blocking strictly
necessary cookies may prevent sign-in or other platform features from working.

Where a consent manager is available, use **Cookie settings** to change optional
choices without deleting your account.

## Changes

This policy will be updated when the technologies used by ScripticX change.
