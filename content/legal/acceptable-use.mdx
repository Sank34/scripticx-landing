ScripticX is a shared learning environment. These rules protect learners,
teachers, the community, and the infrastructure that runs code.

## Be respectful

Do not harass, threaten, impersonate, exploit, or target other people. Do not
post hateful, sexually exploitative, or unlawfully discriminatory content.

Respect intellectual property and privacy. Share only content and personal
information you have the right to share.

## Keep the platform safe

Do not:

- attempt to access accounts, data, or systems without authorization;
- upload malware or use ScripticX to distribute harmful code;
- evade security controls, suspensions, or usage restrictions;
- test vulnerabilities outside the
  [responsible disclosure process](/trust);
- use code execution features to attack ScripticX or third parties.

## Use resources fairly

Automated traffic, scraping, excessive execution workloads, and activity that
degrades service for others may be rate limited or blocked.

Do not use internal or undocumented endpoints as if they were a public API.
Future integrations should use the documented [ScripticX API](/docs/api).

## Enforcement

ScripticX may remove content, limit features, suspend accounts, or preserve
information when reasonably necessary to investigate abuse, protect the
service, or comply with law.

Context and severity matter. Where appropriate, we may provide a warning or an
opportunity to appeal through
[support@scripticx.org](mailto:support@scripticx.org).
