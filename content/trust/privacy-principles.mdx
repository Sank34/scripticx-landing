Privacy should be understandable, not hidden behind legal language. These
principles guide product and engineering decisions at ScripticX.

## Data minimization

We aim to collect only the information needed to provide accounts, learning
features, platform safety, and support. New data collection should have a clear
purpose and an appropriate retention period.

## Purpose and control

Personal information should be used for the purpose communicated when it was
collected. Account and privacy controls should be easy to find and understand.

We do not describe ScripticX as privacy-preserving merely because a policy
exists. Product behavior and operational processes must support the policy.

## Retention

Information should not be retained indefinitely without a reason. Retention may
depend on account status, legal requirements, safety needs, or the time required
to resolve a support request.

## GDPR rights

Depending on location and applicable law, users may have rights to access,
correct, delete, restrict, or export personal information, and to object to
certain processing.

Requests can be sent to
[privacy@scripticx.org](mailto:privacy@scripticx.org). We may need to verify the
requester's identity before acting.
